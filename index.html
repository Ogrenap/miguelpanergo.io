About Miguel!

Miguel Panergo is a professional voice actor based in Vancouver, Canada. With a background of Computer Science, Miguel is a versatile performer who analyzes scripts and characters to truly understand them, connecting them to himself so that he can authentically bring them to life.



Miguel is a graduate from On The Mic Training's Full-Time Voice-Over Program. Through the comprehencive training, he has developed strong fundamentals in animation, commercial, video games, dubbing, ADR, narration and audiobooks. On top of vocal fundamentals and proper mic techniques, Miguel has learned about the business side of the voice over industy, including proper etiquette and professionalism.



Whether it's for work or personal project, Miguel can always be seen having fun with his work and enjoying the process regardless of the context. Once he's comfortable, his enthusiasm comes through and you'll start to wonder where all that energy came from. But that's Miguel for you. Blending in with the crowd and surprising you with his passion when you least expect it.
