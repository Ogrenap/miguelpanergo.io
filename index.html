About Miguel!

Miguel Panergo is a professional voice actor based in Vancouver, Canada. A graduate of On the Mic Training's Comprehensive Program, Miguel’s passion for voice over and versatility as a performer is instantly recognizable in his reads across animation, commercial, video games, dubbing, ADR, narration and audiobooks.



Drawing on all his experiences, including a background in Computer Science, Miguel energizes characters to life with an authentic connection. His professionalism shines through in the studio, alongside a contagious joy in the booth.



Whether it's for work or personal project, Miguel can always be seen having fun with his work and enjoying the process regardless of the context. Once he's comfortable, his enthusiasm comes through and you'll start to wonder where all that energy came from. But that's Miguel for you. Blending in with the crowd and surprising you with his passion when you least expect it.
